Healthcare Institutions

Foundations of Nursing

1. Introduction

Healthcare institutions are organized places where health services are provided to individuals, families and communities. They provide services such as prevention of diseases, diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation and health promotion.

Definition: A healthcare institution is an organization or establishment designed to provide healthcare services to people who need them.

2. Types of Healthcare Institutions

Healthcare institutions can be classified according to the level and type of care they provide.

A. Primary Healthcare Institutions

Primary healthcare institutions provide basic and essential healthcare services and are usually the first point of contact between individuals and the healthcare system.

Examples include:

  • Primary Health Care Centres (PHCs)
  • Health posts
  • Health centres
  • Community health facilities

Services may include health education, immunization, antenatal care, family planning, treatment of minor illnesses and disease prevention.

B. Secondary Healthcare Institutions

Secondary healthcare institutions provide more specialized services than primary healthcare facilities.

Examples include:

  • General hospitals
  • Specialist hospitals at the secondary level
  • District hospitals

They provide services such as medical treatment, surgery, laboratory investigations, maternity services and inpatient care.

C. Tertiary Healthcare Institutions

Tertiary healthcare institutions provide highly specialized and advanced medical services.

Examples include:

  • Teaching hospitals
  • Federal medical centres
  • Highly specialized hospitals

They also provide specialized treatment, advanced diagnostic services, research and training of healthcare professionals.

3. Levels of Healthcare

The healthcare system is commonly organized into three major levels:

  • Primary level – provides basic and essential healthcare.
  • Secondary level – provides specialized healthcare and referral services.
  • Tertiary level – provides highly specialized care, teaching and research.

4. Functions of Healthcare Institutions

Healthcare institutions perform several important functions in maintaining the health of individuals and communities.

  • Promotion of health.
  • Prevention of diseases.
  • Diagnosis of diseases and health conditions.
  • Treatment of illnesses and injuries.
  • Rehabilitation of patients.
  • Provision of emergency care.
  • Provision of maternal and child health services.
  • Health education.
  • Training of healthcare professionals.
  • Conducting healthcare research.

5. Hospital

Definition: A hospital is a healthcare institution established to provide medical, surgical, nursing and other healthcare services to patients.

6. Departments in a Hospital

Hospitals are divided into different departments to allow healthcare workers to provide specialized services.

Common hospital departments include:

  • Medical ward
  • Surgical ward
  • Paediatric ward
  • Maternity/Obstetric department
  • Emergency department
  • Operating theatre
  • Laboratory
  • Pharmacy
  • Radiology/Imaging department
  • Outpatient department
  • Intensive Care Unit

7. Members of the Healthcare Team

Healthcare is provided through teamwork. Different healthcare professionals work together to provide safe and effective patient care.

  • Doctors
  • Nurses
  • Pharmacists
  • Medical laboratory scientists
  • Radiographers
  • Physiotherapists
  • Nutritionists/Dietitians
  • Community health practitioners
  • Dentists
  • Social workers

8. Role of the Nurse in Healthcare Institutions

Nurses play an important role in the healthcare team and provide continuous care to patients.

  • Assessing patients' health conditions.
  • Providing direct nursing care.
  • Administering medications as prescribed.
  • Monitoring vital signs.
  • Maintaining patient safety.
  • Providing health education.
  • Supporting patients emotionally.
  • Maintaining accurate nursing records.
  • Communicating with other members of the healthcare team.
  • Advocating for patients.

9. Referral System

A referral system is the process of transferring or directing a patient from one healthcare facility or level of care to another facility that can provide the required service.

For example, a patient whose condition requires specialized treatment may be referred from a primary healthcare centre to a secondary or tertiary healthcare institution.

Important: The main purpose of referral is to ensure that the patient receives the appropriate level of care required for the condition.

10. Importance of Healthcare Institutions

  • They provide healthcare services.
  • They help prevent and control diseases.
  • They provide emergency treatment.
  • They promote health education.
  • They support maternal and child health.
  • They provide rehabilitation services.
  • They train healthcare professionals.
  • They support medical and healthcare research.

11. Summary

Healthcare institutions are organized establishments that provide healthcare services. They operate at primary, secondary and tertiary levels. Primary healthcare provides basic and essential services, secondary healthcare provides more specialized care, while tertiary healthcare provides highly specialized services, teaching and research. Nurses work with other healthcare professionals as part of the healthcare team to provide safe, effective and patient-centred care.
